Important Student Advisory: Distinguishing the Dayananda Sagar Campuses

Ensure you verify your counselling allotment code and campus location before reporting.

1. DSCE (This Campus)

Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering

• Flagship heritage institution established in 1979.

• Status: Autonomous under VTU Belagavi.

• Location: Shavige Malleshwara Hills, Kumaraswamy Layout, Bengaluru – 560111.

• KCET Code: E034 | COMED-K: E040

2. DSU (Private University)

Dayananda Sagar University

• Established in 2014 as an independent State Private University.

• Status: Not affiliated to VTU (awards DSU degrees).

• Main Campus: Devarakaggalahalli, Harohalli, Kanakapura Road (~40 km south).

3. DSATM (Sister Campus)

Dayananda Sagar Academy of Technology & Mgmt

• Established in 2011 as a sister technical institution.

• Status: Autonomous under VTU Belagavi.

• Location: Kanakapura Road, Udayapura (~18 km south).

• KCET Code: E171
