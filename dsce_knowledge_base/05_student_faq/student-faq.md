# DSCE Student-Contributed FAQ & Campus Guide

This FAQ is written and curated from a student perspective, incorporating authentic peer experiences, discussions from student forums (Reddit, Quora), and everyday campus ground realities at Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering (DSCE), Bengaluru.

---

## 1. Campus Overview, Infrastructure & Facilities

### Q1: Where is DSCE located and how big is the campus?
**A:** DSCE is located in Kumaraswamy Layout (Shavige Malleshwara Hills), South Bengaluru (Pincode: 560111). The campus spans roughly 28 to 30 acres on hilly terrain. It is a shared campus housing several Dayananda Sagar Institutions (DSI), including the engineering college, dental college, pharmacy college, arts/science degree blocks, and business schools.

### Q2: What is the difference between DSCE and DSU?
**A:** DSCE (Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering) is the legacy, established institution founded in 1979; it is an autonomous college affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU). DSU (Dayananda Sagar University) is a private state university established under a separate act, primarily operating out of the newer main campus located in Harohalli on Kanakapura Road. While placement drives are often coordinated across the group, they are distinct academic entities.

### Q3: What are the main buildings and landmarks on campus?
**A:** 
- **Building 1 (Administrative Block):** Houses the Principal's Office, Admissions Hall, Fee Section (Room 206), and administrative offices.
- **Building 2 (Central Library & Heritage Block):** Houses the 4-floor Central Library, Digital Library, and is the primary hub for physical admission document verification and biometric entry.
- **Building 14 (New Architecture / Exam Block):** Houses the Office of the Controller of Examinations (COE).
- **Other Key Blocks:** Dr. Premachandra Sagar Auditorium, CD Sagar Building, Heritage Amphitheatre, and various departmental blocks scattered across the campus.

### Q4: Is there an on-campus ATM and bank?
**A:** Yes. There is a Vijaya Bank / Bank of Baroda branch and an ATM on campus near the main administrative quadrangle, along with nearby State Bank of India (SBI) ATMs located right outside the college gates in Kumaraswamy Layout.

### Q5: What medical facilities exist for emergencies?
**A:** The Sagar Hospitals network and Dayananda Sagar Dental and Medical healthcare centers are located adjacent to or directly within the campus premises. Basic first aid and round-the-clock emergency medical assistance are easily accessible to all students and hostellers.

### Q6: What is the Wi-Fi and internet situation on campus?
**A:** Campus Wi-Fi is available across academic blocks, the digital library, and hostel corridors. Students receive individual login credentials (LDAP/ERP login) after completing their first-year course registration. Connection speeds in the central library and departmental computer labs are generally fast and reliable.

### Q7: Is there a dress code at DSCE?
**A:** There is no strict uniform for standard engineering classes. Students are expected to wear decent, casual attire (collared T-shirts, shirts, jeans). However:
- Mechanical, Electrical, Chemistry, and Workshop laboratories strictly mandate blue workshop lab aprons/coats and closed leather shoes for safety.
- During formal campus placement interview drives and project viva-voce presentations, strict formal attire (formals and dress shoes) is required.

## 2. Hostels, Accommodation & Mess

### Q8: What are the different on-campus hostel options?
**A:**
- **Indian Boys Hostel:** Located on campus. Features older multi-occupancy rooms (2 to 4 sharing) with shared washrooms. It is the most economical on-campus option.
- **NRI Boys Hostel:** Offers upgraded accommodation (primarily double or single sharing), often with attached washrooms, better furnishings, and higher upkeep at a premium fee.
- **Girls Hostel:** Secured hostel blocks situated on campus with dedicated female security guards and resident wardens.

### Q9: What are the hostel curfew and gate closing timings?
**A:**
- **Girls Hostel:** Enforces a strict curfew of **9:00 PM** (some blocks allow up to 9:30 PM with prior warden permission). Biometric and register sign-ins are strictly taken.
- **Boys Hostel:** Enforces a curfew of **10:00 PM**.
- Missing curfew without documented prior approval results in disciplinary warnings, fines, or calls to registered parents/guardians.

### Q10: How do hostellers get night-out or weekend leave permission?
**A:** Students must apply for an Outpass through the hostel ERP portal or warden office at least 24 hours in advance. Weekend night-outs generally require SMS/email confirmation from the parent’s registered contact number before the outpass is activated.

### Q11: What is the mess food like?
**A:** The mess provides 4 meals a day: Breakfast, Lunch, Evening Snacks with Tea/Coffee, and Dinner. The menu features a mix of South Indian and North Indian food (idli, dosa, rice, sambar, dal, rotis, seasonal vegetables, paneer once/twice a week). While nutritious and hygienic, most students find the food monotonous after a few months.
- On Sundays and holidays, mess timings are curtailed, and on alternate Sunday evenings, dinner may be skipped or light, encouraging students to explore local eateries.

### Q12: Can students easily find off-campus PGs or flats nearby?
**A:** Yes. Kumaraswamy Layout (1st Stage and 2nd Stage), ISRO Layout, and Padmanabhanagar are packed with student paying-guest (PG) accommodations for both boys and girls. Rents range between Rs. 6,500 and Rs. 14,000 per month depending on sharing, food inclusion, and attached bathroom facilities. Many 2nd, 3rd, and 4th-year students rent 2BHK/3BHK apartments in nearby residential lanes.

## 3. Canteen, Food Culture & Campus Hangouts

### Q13: What food options and canteens are available on campus?
**A:**
- **Indian Canteen:** Serves pocket-friendly South Indian snacks (masala dosa, vada, curd rice, meals) and standard vegetarian lunches.
- **NRI Canteen / Food Court:** Offers broader multi-cuisine menus including fried rice, noodles, rolls, pasta, and fresh fruit juices.
- **Nescafe Kiosks:** Multiple kiosks across campus serving coffee, tea, Maggi, iced tea, and packaged snacks.
- **Juice Stalls:** Popular juice bars located near the sports ground and basketball courts.

### Q14: Where do students hang out right outside campus?
**A:**
- **Kumaraswamy Layout 50 Feet Road:** Lined with budget food stalls, tea points, shawarma outlets, chaat shops, roll points, and bakeries.
- **Kadirenahalli Cross:** Known for bakeries, South Indian restaurants, and fast-food eateries.
- **Banashankari BDA Complex:** A popular shopping and food hub roughly 10 minutes away by bus or auto.

## 4. Academics, Attendance & Examination Rules

### Q15: What is the autonomous examination system at DSCE?
**A:** DSCE operates as an autonomous institution affiliated with VTU. The college designs its own syllabus, schedules its own academic calendar, sets internal and final examination papers, and evaluates answer scripts in-house through the Controller of Examinations (COE) in Building 14. However, the final degree certificate is conferred by Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), Belagavi.

### Q16: What is the mandatory attendance requirement?
**A:** Officially, VTU autonomous regulations require **85% attendance** in every theory and practical subject. A relaxation down to **75%** is permitted only on valid medical grounds (with verified doctor certificates) or official college duty representation (sports, cultural fests, hackathons).

### Q17: What happens if attendance drops below 75%?
**A:** If your attendance in a course falls below 75%, you will be placed on the **NSAR (Not Satisfied Attendance Requirement)** list. You will be barred from writing the Semester End Examination (SEE) for that specific subject, receiving an 'NE' grade. You must re-register for the course during the supplementary or fast-track summer semester by paying the requisite course fee.

### Q18: How is the grading system structured (CIE vs. SEE)?
**A:** Total marks for each course are divided equally:
- **Continuous Internal Evaluation (CIE):** 50% weightage. Derived from 3 Internal Assessment Tests (IATs), weekly laboratory evaluations, surprise quizzes, and course assignments.
- **Semester End Examination (SEE):** 50% weightage. Comprehensive written or practical university exam conducted at the end of the semester.
- Students must score a minimum qualifying percentage in both CIE and SEE independently to pass each subject.

### Q19: What is the IAT (Internal Assessment Test) schedule?
**A:** There are three IATs held per semester:
- **IAT-1:** Roughly 4–6 weeks after semester commencement.
- **IAT-2:** Around the 10th–12th week.
- **IAT-3:** Held close to the end of term (14th–16th week).
The best 2 out of 3 IAT scores (or a weighted combination depending on current department regulations) are computed toward the final CIE mark.

### Q20: What are Fast Track / Supplementary exams?
**A:** Fast Track / Summer Semesters are held after the even semester results are declared (typically June–August). Students with backlogs ('F' or 'NE' grades) can re-register for up to 3–4 subjects, attend fast-track classes, retake internal tests, and clear their arrears without losing a full academic year.

## 5. Placements, Internships & Skill Development

### Q21: How are placements conducted across branches?
**A:** Placements are centrally managed by the Central Placement Division in coordination with the Centre for Innovation & Leadership (CIL). Companies visit the campus starting from the 6th/7th semester. Tech and circuital branches (CSE, ISE, AI&ML, ECE) receive the bulk of IT, product, and consulting company drives, while core branches (Mechanical, Civil, Chemical) see selective core recruiting alongside mass IT eligibility.

### Q22: What are the highest and average salary packages?
**A:**
- **Highest CTC:** Ranges from 25 LPA to 45+ LPA (achieved by top programmers in tier-1 product companies like Amazon, Cisco, PhonePe, or off-campus pool drives).
- **Average CTC for CSE/ISE/Tech branches:** Ranges between 6.5 LPA and 8.5 LPA.
- **Overall College Average CTC:** Typically 4.5 LPA to 6.0 LPA.
- **Mass Recruiter CTC:** Typically 3.5 LPA to 4.5 LPA (TCS, Infosys, Accenture, Wipro, Cognizant).

### Q23: What minimum CGPA is required to be eligible for placements?
**A:** While 6.0 CGPA qualifies for mass recruiters, almost all Tier-1 and product companies mandate:
- **7.5 or 8.0+ CGPA** with **no active backlogs** across all completed semesters.
- Maintaining an **8.5+ CGPA** ensures eligibility for 90%+ of visiting companies.

### Q24: What is the Centre for Innovation & Leadership (CIL)?
**A:** CIL is DSCE’s internal training division that runs mandatory soft-skills, communication, aptitude, and verbal reasoning workshops starting from the 2nd and 3rd years to prepare students for campus placement aptitude rounds and HR interviews.

### Q25: Are internships mandatory for UG students?
**A:** Yes. Under autonomous VTU regulations, all B.E. students must complete mandatory industry internships (typically during the summer breaks after the 4th and 6th semesters or in the 8th semester) and submit an evaluation report and viva-voce for academic credits.

## 6. Technical Societies, Clubs & Hackathons

### Q26: What is Point Blank (PB) and why is it famous?
**A:** Point Blank (`pointblank.club`) is DSCE's premier student-run technical and coding society. It is renowned across India for:
- Winning multiple consecutive Smart India Hackathon (SIH) championships.
- High numbers of Google Summer of Code (GSoC) scholars and LFX mentees.
- Organizing **Zenith**, one of Bangalore's most competitive student hackathons.
- Running internal CP contests (*PB Hustle*) and CTF cybersecurity events.
Recruitment occurs annually through a competitive test and interview process.

### Q27: What other technical clubs exist?
**A:**
- **IEEE DSCE Student Branch:** Very active branch organizing paper presentations, workshops, and international conferences.
- **GDSC (Google Developer Student Clubs):** Focuses on Android, Web, Flutter, and Google Cloud workshops.
- **Aero Club & DSI Robotics Club:** Builds autonomous drones, RC planes, and battle-bots for national aero-design and SAE competitions.
- **Quest Quiz Club:** One of Bengaluru's top college quizzing circuits.

### Q28: What cultural and non-technical clubs can I join?
**A:**
- **Dance Crews & Music Societies:** Western and classical music and dance teams that represent DSCE across college festivals.
- **Dramatics / Theatre Societies:** Active in street plays, one-act plays, and mime.
- **Rotaract Club DSCE:** Social service, blood donation camps, and community outreach.
- **NSS / NCC Units:** Mandatory community service and personality development units for AICTE activity points.

## 7. College Fests & Campus Celebrations

### Q29: What is the flagship annual college fest?
**A:** **FURORE** is DSCE’s flagship inter-collegiate cultural fest, usually celebrated between February and April. It spans 2–3 days and draws thousands of students from colleges across Bangalore for fashion shows, celebrity DJ nights, rock band battles, street dancing, and gaming tournaments.

### Q30: What is Taranga / Sagar Utsav?
**A:** **Taranga** and **Sagar Utsav** are intra-college celebrations and Kannada Rajyotsava festivals highlighting regional arts, music, dance, folk performances, and cultural heritage on the Heritage Amphitheatre stage.

### Q31: How do students earn AICTE Activity Points through events?
**A:** Under AICTE rules, every B.E. student must accumulate 100 activity points (75 for lateral entry) through participation in NSS activities, blood donation drives, fest organization, sports, tree plantation, or rural community development to qualify for degree award. Certificates are submitted to departmental coordinators in each odd semester.

## 8. Commute, Metro & Public Transportation

### Q32: What is the nearest Namma Metro station to DSCE?
**A:**
- **Banashankari Metro Station / TTMC (Green Line):** Roughly 3.5 km away. The primary transport hub with frequent connecting feeder buses directly to the campus.
- **Konanakunte Cross Metro Station (Green Line):** Roughly 3.0 km away along Kanakapura Road.
- **Yelachenahalli Metro Station (Green Line):** Roughly 3.5 km away.

### Q33: Which BMTC bus routes go directly to DSCE?
**A:**
- **Route 15-E:** Runs directly from Kempegowda Bus Station (Majestic) / City Market to Banashankari and drops students directly at the **Dayananda Sagar College Gate** in Kumaraswamy Layout.
- **210 Series Buses (e.g., 210-R, 210-U):** Connect to **Kadirenahalli Cross**, located a brief 5-minute walk from the college.

### Q34: Is parking available on campus for student vehicles?
**A:** Two-wheeler parking is designated inside dedicated parking lots near the main gates and sports grounds upon showing a valid college ID card. Four-wheeler student parking inside campus is strictly regulated and often restricted during working hours due to limited space; most students park cars along designated roads in Kumaraswamy Layout.

## 9. Administrative Procedures & Student Hacks

### Q35: Where do I pay semester and hostel fees?
**A:** Tuition and college fees are paid online through the official payment portal (`dsce.edu.in/admissions/pay-fee-online`) or at the **Fee Section** (Building No. 1, 2nd Floor, Room No. 206) using debit card, UPI, or NEFT/RTGS challans. **Cash is never accepted.**

### Q36: What should I do if I lose my Student ID card?
**A:** Report the loss immediately to the Admissions / Reception office in Building 1. You will be asked to pay a nominal ID replacement fee (usually Rs. 200–500) at the fee counter and submit a replacement slip to receive a newly issued RFID card.

### Q37: How do I access library resources and study zones?
**A:** Your Student ID card acts as your library membership card with embedded RFID. 
- The **Circulation Section** operates from 9:00 AM to 5:30 PM on all working days.
- The **Reference Section and Private Study Zone** remain open until **10:00 PM** on weekdays, making it the quietest place on campus to study for exams.
- Digital Library computers provide free access to IEEE Xplore, ScienceDirect, and VTU consortium journals.

### Q38: Who do I contact in case of harassment or ragging?
**A:** Ragging is strictly prohibited. You can immediately reach out to:
- **Anti-Ragging Committee Chairman:** Dr. H K Ramaraju (HOD Civil, `hod-cvl@dayanandasagar.edu`)
- **Women Empowerment & Grievance Cell:** Dr. Hemalatha K G (`hod-mba-vtu@dayanandasagar.edu`)
- **First Year Student Office:** Phone `080-42161763`, email `firstyeardsce@gmail.com`
- Direct emergency police protection is supported by the Kumaraswamy Layout Police Station located nearby.

### Q39: What is the best strategy to maintain a high CGPA at DSCE?
**A:**
1. **Never skip Continuous Internal Evaluations (CIE):** Internals make up 50% of your grade. Maximizing your IAT scores makes getting an 'S' or 'A' grade in the Semester End Exam (SEE) significantly easier.
2. **Protect your attendance:** Stay above 80% at all times to have a buffer before exam season.
3. **Target laboratory marks:** Lab internals and vivas offer high-scoring credits; submit observation notebooks and records on time every week.
