# NAAC Accreditation and IQAC

## NAAC status (as published on the IQAC page)
- DSCE has been accredited by NAAC under **Cycle II** with a CGPA of **3.20** on a four-point scale at **'A' grade**, valid for five years until **27 June 2027**.
- An earlier accreditation recorded CGPA **3.42** at 'A' grade, valid up to 10 July 2021.
- The NAAC peer team's final report recommended that DSCE set up an IQAC. NAAC revised IQAC guidelines in October 2013, and institutions must submit an Annual Quality Assurance Report (AQAR) every year. A functional IQAC and timely AQAR submission are minimum institutional requirements for subsequent accreditation cycles.
- NAAC certificate page: https://www.dsce.edu.in/documents/accreditations/nacc-certificate

## IQAC objectives
- Develop a system for conscious, consistent and catalytic action to improve academic and administrative performance.
- Promote quality enhancement through internalised quality culture and best practices.

## IQAC mechanisms and functions
Ensuring timely, efficient performance of academic, administrative and financial tasks; relevance and quality of academic and research programmes; equitable access and affordability; modern teaching and learning methods; credible evaluation procedures; adequacy of support services; research sharing and networking. Functions include quality benchmarks, a learner-centric environment, feedback from students, parents and stakeholders, workshops and seminars on quality, documentation of programmes, institutional database through MIS, and preparation of the AQAR for NAAC.

## IQAC leadership (as listed)
Chairperson: Dr. B G Prasad, Principal. Convener: Dr. Shobha K R (Dean IQAC, HOD-ECE). Main coordinator: Dr. Trupti Tagarae (Asst. Prof. ECE; spelling as printed on the source page). Members include HODs and Vice Principals, senior administrative officers, student, alumni, industry and external academician nominees, and department IQAC coordinators.
