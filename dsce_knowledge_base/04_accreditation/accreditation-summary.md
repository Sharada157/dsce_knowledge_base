# Accreditation and Approvals Summary

- DSCE is an Autonomous Institute affiliated to Visvesvaraya Technological University (VTU), approved by AICTE and UGC.
- Accredited by NAAC with an 'A' grade. The About page states that 12 UG programs are accredited by NBA (National Board of Accreditation). The Information Science and Engineering department page states its B.E. program has been autonomous under VTU since 2015 and is NBA accredited.
