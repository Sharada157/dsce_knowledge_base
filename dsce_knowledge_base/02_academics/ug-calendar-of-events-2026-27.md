# UG Calendar of Events 2026-27 (odd semesters)

The calendar is subject to modification. Dates marked tentative are from the source document's notes.

## 1st Semester (first-year B.E., 2026-27)
- 24 Aug to 4 Sep 2026: Students Induction Program.
- 24 Aug to 3 Sep 2026: Course registration for odd semester; registration of AICTE Activity Points.
- 7 Sep 2026: Commencement of classes.
- 14 Oct 2026 to 17 Oct 2026: Internal Assessment Test 1 (IAT-1). 17 Oct is a working day; IAT-1 is scheduled in line with the NBA committee's visit to DSCE.
- 23 Oct 2026: Display of IAT-1 marks.
- 23 Nov to 26 Nov 2026: Internal Assessment Test 2 (IAT-2).
- IAT-2 marks display: the source lists both 12 Nov and 3 Dec; 3 Dec is consistent with IAT-2 being held 23-26 Nov.
- 18 Dec to 26 Dec 2026: Laboratory internal assessment, skill lab assessment, course end survey.
- 28 Dec to 31 Dec 2026: Internal Assessment Test 3 (IAT-3).
- 6 Jan 2027: Display of IAT-3 marks.
- 7 Jan 2027: Submission of CIE marks and attendance to the COE office; last working day.
- Total working days: 101.
- Semester End Examinations (tentative): Lab 11 Jan to 25 Jan 2027; Theory 27 Jan to 8 Feb 2027.
- Even semester commences 15 Feb 2027 (tentative).

## 3rd and 5th Semester (2026-27)
- 24 Aug to 28 Aug 2026: Course registration for odd semester and commencement of classes. 3rd sem: registration of AICTE Activity Points, NCMC courses and Skill Enhancement Courses (SEC). 5th sem: registration of NPTEL/MOOCs as per the scheme.
- 28 Sep to 30 Sep 2026: IAT-1. Marks displayed 7 Oct.
- 3 Nov to 5 Nov 2026: IAT-2. Marks displayed 12 Nov.
- 8 Dec to 10 Dec 2026: IAT-3. Marks displayed 15 Dec.
- 12 Dec to 17 Dec 2026: Laboratory internal assessment. 14 to 16 Dec: course end survey.
- 18 Dec 2026: Submission of CIE marks and attendance to the COE office, submission of NCMC/SEC marks, last working day.
- Total working days: 86.
- Semester End Examinations: from 21 Dec 2026 (tentative).

## 7th Semester (2026-27)
- 20 Jul to 23 Jul 2026: Course registration for odd semester; classes commence 20 Jul; registration for NPTEL/MOOCs.
- 19 Aug to 21 Aug 2026: IAT-1. Marks displayed 27 Aug.
- 23 Sep to 25 Sep 2026: IAT-2. Marks displayed 30 Sep.
- 21 Oct to 24 Oct 2026: Major Project Phase-1 evaluation (as per rubrics).
- 30 Oct to 2 Nov 2026: IAT-3.
- 3 Nov to 6 Nov 2026: Evaluation of MOOCs, laboratory internal assessment, course end survey. IAT-3 marks displayed 6 Nov.
- 9 Nov 2026: Submission of CIE marks, attendance, NPTEL/MOOC certificates and NCMC marks to the COE office; last working day.
- Total working days: 84.
- Semester End Examinations from 11 Nov (tentative). The source prints the year as 2027, which is likely a typo for 2026.
