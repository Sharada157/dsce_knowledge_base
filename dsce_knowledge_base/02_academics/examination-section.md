# Examination Section (Controller of Examinations)

The Controller of Examinations (COE) is assisted by the Deputy Controller of Examination and staff for conducting examinations and publishing results on time. The section is responsible for conduct of examinations, valuation of answer scripts, and publication of results and certificates.

A manual titled **Academic Rules and Regulations** has been compiled with all rules, regulations and procedures. The rules governing academic and assessment evaluation are explained to students during the induction program every year.

The examination process is automated: question paper setting, digital depository of answer scripts and digital evaluation. Orientation sessions on examination rules are held regularly for faculty. The section works with the affiliating university (VTU).
