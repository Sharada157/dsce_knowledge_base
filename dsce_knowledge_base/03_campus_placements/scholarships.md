# Scholarships

Apply through the National Scholarship Portal (NSP: https://scholarships.gov.in/) or the Karnataka State Scholarship Portal (SSP: https://ssp.postmatric.karnataka.gov.in/). For institute-level help contact the Scholarship Section: 080-26662226 ext 116, scholarship-dsce@dayanandasagar.edu.

| # | Scholarship | Sponsor | Eligibility / how to apply |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | AIC-DSU Foundation Scholarships: Research Scholars and Scientific Officers | NITI Aayog (GoI) and Dayananda Sagar University | Master's degree in Science/Engineering, wishing to pursue a PhD; research/industry experience preferred. Details: https://www.dsce.edu.in/images/AIC-DSU_CONTENT.pdf |
| 2 | Post Matric Scholarship Scheme for Minorities | Ministry of Minority Affairs, GoI | Six notified minority communities (Muslim, Christian, Sikh, Buddhist, Jain, Parsi) meeting scheme guidelines. Apply via NSP. |

| 5 | Scholarships for Top Class Education for students with disabilities | Dept. of Empowerment of Persons with Disabilities | As above. |
