# Placements at DSCE

The centralised placement division facilitates placements for all students (UG and PG). It provides pre-placement soft-skills training in association with CIL (Centre for Innovation & Leadership) and the HR Division, and supports training in CV writing, aptitude assessment, mock interviews and group discussions. Internship opportunities in various companies are provided for interested UG/PG students.

Infrastructure supports pre-placement talks, presentations, online/written tests, group discussions/JMT and interviews through a well-equipped auditorium/seminar hall and rooms for training, interviews and counselling. The Placement Cell coordinates with recruiters end to end.

Talent available for recruitment/internships: BE/M.Tech (CSE, ISE, ECE, EEE, Medical Electronics, Telecom, Instrumentation, Mechanical, IE&M, Civil, Construction Technology & Management, Chemical, Biotechnology, Architecture, Automobile, Aeronautical); MCA, BCA, BSc (CS), BCom, BBM, MCom and other programs across the Dayananda Sagar group; Diploma (CSE/ISE/Mech/ECE/EEE/Civil).

The list of recruitment partners is shown as an image on the page (company names not available as text). Department placement pages list company-wise results, e.g. https://www.dsce.edu.in/aeronautical-engineering/aero-placements
