## About
- Collection of over a lakh books and e-books, CDs, DVDs, plus latest journals and periodicals.
- Accessible to all undergraduates, postgraduates, PhD scholars and faculty. E-books and e-journals are available through the Digital Library on dedicated systems. A mini auditorium supports seminars and presentations.
- Services: membership, circulation, book bank, reference services, light reading zone, access control, AV auditorium reservation, inter-library loan, user awareness, print zone, plagiarism checking tool, RFID system, OPAC search (https://dsceopac.libsys10.in/), Kindle e-books, NDL access.
