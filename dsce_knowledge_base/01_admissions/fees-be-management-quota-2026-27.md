## Payment
All payments by Demand Draft (DD) in favour of Dayananda Sagar College of Engineering after confirmation by the Management, or online (details provided later).

## Minimum academic qualifications (as printed in this document)
- 10th Std: minimum 70% in Maths and Science subjects separately.
- 12th Std pre-board exam: 65% in Maths and Physics subjects separately.
- Candidates must appear in one of CET / COMEDK / AIEEE / JEE.
- Candidates from non-Indian boards must obtain an AIU (Association of Indian Universities) equivalence certificate from New Delhi.

## Documents (carry soft and hard copies)
Originals of 10th and 12th; Transfer Certificate; Migration Certificate (non-Karnataka); photocopy of parents' PAN card; three sets of photocopies; scanned originals (colour, PDF, within 1 MB) and a passport photo soft copy within 500 KB.
Send scanned documents with a mobile number by email: admissions@dayanandasagar.edu for all CS branches, ISE, AI&ML, Robotics, Aeronautical, Biotechnology and ECE; query@dayanandasagar.edu for other branches.
