# PG Admission Process and Fees 2026-27, PGCET Quota (document dated 29-09-2026)

## Common admission process (MBA, MCA, M.Tech)
1. **Step 1:** Download the KEA confirmation slip from the KEA student login, print it, and report to the Main Library (Building No. 2) for biometric attendance and the Admission Order copy.
2. **Step 2:** Scan each original document separately in colour using a dedicated scanner (not a phone camera), JPG, each within 1 MB; keep a passport photo soft copy within 500 KB. Upload through the student login.
3. **Step 3:** Submit the allotment letter with the application number at the Fee Section, Building No. 1, 2nd Floor, Room No. 206. Pay by debit card, UPI or NEFT/RTGS and collect the challan. **Cash is not accepted.**
4. **Step 4:** Print the application using the fee challan, submit originals and collect the acknowledgement at Building No. 2, Main Library.

## Fee structure (INR)
| Program (PGCET) | Tuition already paid at KEA | College/VTU fees (A) | Eligibility fee (B) | Total at DSCE: studied in Karnataka | Total at DSCE: studied outside Karnataka |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MBA | 69,630 | 45,026 | 0 or 1,800 | **45,026** | **46,826** |
| MCA | 69,630 | 34,026 | 0 or 1,800 | **34,026** | **35,826** |
| M.Tech | 81,180 | 45,326 | 0 or 1,800 | **45,326** | **47,126** |

The eligibility fee of Rs. 1,800 applies to students who studied outside Karnataka.

## Documents to submit
1. KEA confirmation slip with fee-paid receipt
2. Originals of 10th, 12th and all UG (all semesters) marks cards
3. Transfer Certificate / PDC / Convocation certificate
4. Migration Certificate for students from boards other than the State Board
5. Hyderabad Karnataka (KKR) claim documents, if applicable
6. Photocopy of parent's PAN / Aadhaar card
7. Three sets of black-and-white photocopies
8. Scanned originals in JPEG (within 2 MB) and a passport photo soft copy within 500 KB
9. Students who studied outside India: passport and visa copy
