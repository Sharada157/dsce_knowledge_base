# 1st Year B.E. 2026-27: KEA / CET Quota (document dated 15-07-2026)

## Admission process
1. **Step 1:** Download the KEA confirmation slip from the KEA student login and print it. Report at the Main Library (Building #2) and complete biometric attendance to receive the Admission Order copy.
2. **Step 2:** Scan all original documents separately in colour (JPG/JPEG, each within 1 MB) using a document scanner (not a mobile camera), and keep a passport-size colour photo soft copy within 500 KB. Do the student data entry in the student login and note the Application Number.
3. **Step 3:** Produce the allotment letter with the application number at the Fee Section (Building No. 1, 2nd floor, Room No. 206). Fees can be paid by debit card, UPI or NEFT/RTGS. Collect the fee challan.
4. **Step 4:** Get the application printed by producing the fee challan. Submit all original documents and obtain the acknowledgement at Building No. 2, Main Library.

## Documents to submit at admission
1. KEA confirmation slip with fee-paid receipt
2. Originals of 10th and 12th (PUC) marks cards
3. Transfer Certificate
4. Non-Karnataka students: Migration Certificate / Study Certificate
5. Caste, category, Karnataka Kalyana Region (KKR), rural, Kannada medium certificates (if any)
6. Parents' PAN card / Aadhaar card (soft copy)
7. Student's passport-size colour photo, signature and Aadhaar card (soft copy)
8. Three sets of photocopies of the above
9. Students who studied outside India: copy of passport and visa

Soft copies of items 1 to 7 must be uploaded in the college portal.
