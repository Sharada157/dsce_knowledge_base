# Admissions Overview

**Institution codes:** CET Code E007 | COMEDK Code E040 | PGCET MBA B158 | PGCET M.Tech T822 | PGCET MCA C422

Admission to DSCE is through CET (Karnataka) and COMEDK. The process commences in March every year and concludes on 31 July of the same year. Lateral entry (Diploma quota) through CET is for 3rd semester and commences in July/August. Admission enquiry form: https://apply.dayanandasagar.edu/engineering-dsce

## B.E. programs (UG)
- Admission is through the Common Entrance Test (CET) conducted by the Karnataka Government (KEA) and COMEDK. Students are considered by merit ranking.
- A pass in PUC / 12th / 10+2 or equivalent in the first attempt, with Physics and Mathematics compulsory and English as one of the subjects/languages.
- Minimum 45% aggregate in Physics, Mathematics and one of Chemistry / Biology / Computer Science / Electronics.
- Karnataka SC, ST and other backward classes (Cat-1, 2A, 2B, 3A, 3B): minimum 40% aggregate in the optional subjects.

## M.Tech programs (PG)
- Pass in B.E. / B.Tech or equivalent.
- A written test is conducted by the university and a rank list is prepared; admission is by single-window counselling.
- Candidates with a valid GATE score get first preference in seat selection, then university rank holders.

## MBA program (PG)
- Pass in the prescribed qualifying degree with at least 50% aggregate across all years/semesters. PGCET score is mandatory for Government Quota students.
- SC/ST and other Government of Karnataka classified groups: at least 45% aggregate.

## MCA program (PG)
- Same eligibility as MBA: at least 50% aggregate (45% for SC/ST and notified groups); PGCET score mandatory for Government Quota.
- The revised MCA course is two years / four semesters.

## M.Sc. (Engg.) / Ph.D.
- Ph.D.: admission through synopsis selection, ULRAT test and an interview conducted by VTU for affiliated research centres. Requires a Master's degree from a recognised university or equivalent.
- All eligible applicants take a written entrance test, which must be cleared to attend the interview. Admission is based on interview performance.
- M.Sc. (Engg.): Bachelor's degree in the respective field; the rest of the procedure is the same as for Ph.D.
- Ph.D. full-time admission application form (PDF): https://www.dsce.edu.in/images/Research/ApplicationPhD.pdf

## Management quota
- **Engineering:** minimum 50% aggregate in Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics (PCM), each subject passed individually. (See the 2026-27 management quota fee document for stricter criteria listed there.)
- **MBA:** minimum 50% aggregate in the qualifying degree; students who took PGCET, MAT, CAT or KMAT are eligible on merit.
