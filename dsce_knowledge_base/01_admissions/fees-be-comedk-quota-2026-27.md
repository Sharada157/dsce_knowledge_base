# 1st Year B.E. 2026-27: COMED-K Quota (document dated 15-07-2026)

## Admission process
1. **Step 1:** Obtain the allotment letter from COMED-K and report at DSCE Building No. 02, Main Library for verification of the admission order and original documents.
2. **Step 2:** Wait for your turn to complete the online entry. Scan originals in colour (JPEG, each within 1 MB) and keep a passport photo soft copy within 500 KB ready (avoid mobile scanning).
3. **Step 3:** Produce the allotment letter with the application number at the Fee Section (Building No. 1, 2nd floor, Room No. 206). Pay by debit card, UPI or NEFT/RTGS and collect the fee challan.
4. **Step 4:** Get the application printed with the fee challan, submit all originals, and obtain the acknowledgement at Building No. 2, Main Library.

## Documents to submit at admission
1. Original COMEDK allotment letter with COMEDK fee-paid receipt
2. Originals of 10th and 12th marks cards
3. Transfer Certificate
4. Non-Karnataka students: Migration Certificate / Study Certificate
5. Karnataka Kalyana Region claim: relevant documents
6. Parents' PAN card / Aadhaar card (soft copy)
7. Student's passport photo, signature and Aadhaar card (soft copy)
8. Three sets of photocopies
9. Students who studied outside India: passport and visa copy
